# Appointment Documents Structure

## Core Concept
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.4.6, information management requirements are formally bound into commercial appointments through a structured suite of annexes, establishing contractual enforceability and hierarchy of terms.

## Mandatory Appointment Annexes
1. **Exchange Information Requirements (EIR)**: Defining client or lead party information deliverables.
2. **Project Information Standard**: Technical constraints, naming rules, classification, and formats.
3. **Project Information Production Methods & Procedures**: Mandatory processes and QA gates.
4. **Information Protocol**: Governing CDE rights, digital copyright, data liability, and security.
5. **Confirmed Delivery Team BEP**: Agreed technical and management execution baseline.
6. **Master Information Delivery Plan (MIDP)** or **TIDP**: Scheduled delivery baseline with milestone dates.
