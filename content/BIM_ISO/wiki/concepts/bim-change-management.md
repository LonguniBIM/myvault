# BIM Change Management & Golden Thread

Driving systemic digital change in the built environment requires overcoming legacy adversarial procurement, fragmented supply chains, and low margins through policy leadership and clear legal frameworks.

## Catalysts for Industry Change
1. **The Latham Report (1994, *Constructing the Team*)**: First major identification of adversarial culture; urged client leadership and teamwork.
2. **The Egan Report (1998, *Rethinking Construction*)**: Targeted 10% annual cost/time reduction and 20% defect reduction through standardized processes.
3. **The Farmer Review (2016, *Modernise or Die*)**: Diagnosed critical industry symptoms including demographic shrinkage, low productivity, and financial fragility.
4. **The Hackitt Report (2018, *Building a Safer Future*)**: Investigated the Grenfell Tower disaster; mandated the **Golden Thread of Information** to guarantee an unbroken, digital record of building design, construction, and life-safety decisions.
