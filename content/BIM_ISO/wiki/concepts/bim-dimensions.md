# BIM Dimensions (3D to 7D)

**BIM Dimensions** categorize the enrichment of building information models with specific data types to serve defined project lifecycle use cases:

| Dimension | Core Information | Primary Applications | Supporting Tools / Schemas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3D** | Geometry & Spatial Data | Clash detection, visualization, parametric modeling | Revit, ArchiCAD, IFC |
| **4D** | Time & Schedule | Construction sequencing, logistics planning, site simulation | Synchro, Navisworks TimeLiner |
| **5D** | Cost & Quantities | Quantity takeoff (QTO), real-time budget tracking | CostX, Solibri, Vico |
| **6D** | Sustainability & Energy | Embodied carbon (EPD), energy simulation, daylight analysis | H\B:ERT, SimScale, BREEAM |
| **7D** | Facilities Management | O&M manuals, warranty tracking, lifecycle maintenance | COBie, CAFM, IWMS, AIM |
