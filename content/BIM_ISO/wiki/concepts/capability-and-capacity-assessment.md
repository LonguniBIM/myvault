# Capability and Capacity Assessment

## Core Concept
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.3.3, a prospective delivery team must evaluate both **Capability** (competence, skills, tools, experience) and **Capacity** (available human resources, IT infrastructure, time allocation) across all task teams before submitting a tender.

## Assessment Dimensions
1. **Competency & Skills**: Knowledge of ISO 19650 workflows, discipline authoring software, IFC schema mapping, and Uniclass classification.
2. **Resource Availability (Capacity)**: Availability of named key personnel during peak project delivery milestones.
3. **IT & Hardware Infrastructure**: Computing hardware specifications, network bandwidth, CDE compatibility, and software license availability.
4. **Gap Identification & Mitigation**: Identifying skill or resource deficiencies that must be addressed during the mobilization phase.
