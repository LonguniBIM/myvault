# Cost of Poor Information & Rework

Empirical studies across the global construction industry demonstrate that unmanaged, uncoordinated documentation is the leading root cause of project cost overruns, delays, and safety hazards.

## Empirical Metrics of Industry Inefficiency

| Waste Factor | Industry Benchmark Metric | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Site Rework** | **Up to 30% of construction cost** | Direct financial waste correcting clashes and misaligned components on site. |
| **Material Waste** | **At least 10% of delivered materials** | Scrap from offcuts and unstandardized, uncoordinated detailing. |
| **Labour Underutilization** | **Labour efficiency limited to 40–60%** | Workers standing idle waiting for design clarifications (RFIs) or delayed materials. |
| **Accidents & Health/Safety** | **3–6% of total project cost** | Site delays, plant damage, and regulatory enforcement investigations. |
| **Total Non-Value Adding Work** | **Over 50% of total project expenditure** | Projects costing significantly more than necessary due to avoidable errors. |

## The Role of ISO 19650
Structured information management, early spatial coordination via federated models, and disciplined CDE workflows are explicitly designed to eliminate this 50% non-value-adding waste.
