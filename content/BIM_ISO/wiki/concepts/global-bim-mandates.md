# Global BIM Mandates & Adoption

Across the globe, governments and major client organizations have enacted statutory mandates requiring digital information management for public infrastructure and buildings:

| Region / Nation | Mandate Year | Core Policy / Scope | Primary Standards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **United Kingdom** | 2016 (Centrally Procured) | Mandated BIM Level 2 on all central government capital projects; now transitioned to UK BIM Framework / ISO 19650. | ISO 19650, BS 8541, Uniclass 2015 |
| **Scandinavia (Norway, Finland, Sweden)** | 2007–2015 | Pioneered public sector OpenBIM mandates; compulsory IFC exchange for public estates (Statsbygg, Senate Properties). | ISO 16739 (IFC), OpenBIM |
| **Singapore** | 2015 | Building and Construction Authority (BCA) mandated electronic BIM submissions for regulatory approval. | BCA e-Submission, IFC |
| **France** | 2017 | Plan BIM 2022 digitalizing public infrastructure works. | ISO 19650, AFNOR |
| **UAE (Dubai)** | 2014 | Mandatory BIM for buildings over 40 storeys or 300,000 sq. ft. | Dubai Municipality Circulars |
