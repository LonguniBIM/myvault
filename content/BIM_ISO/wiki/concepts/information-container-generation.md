# Information Container Generation

## Core Concept
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.6.1, information generation is performed by task teams strictly within the **Work In Progress (WIP)** CDE state. Information containers must adhere to standard naming syntax, spatial boundaries, and agreed Level of Information Need.

## Core Rules
1. **WIP Isolation**: Unchecked WIP containers are strictly internal to the authoring task team and must not be referenced by external disciplines.
2. **Reference Integrity**: Before authoring, modelers must check and link the latest approved containers from the CDE Shared state.
3. **Parametric & Data Alignment**: Geometric models must incorporate required alphanumeric attributes and Uniclass 2015 classification tokens.
