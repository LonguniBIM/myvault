# Information Delivery Milestones

Under **ISO 19650-2 §5.1.3**, **Information Delivery Milestones** are scheduled delivery dates when specific information deliverables must be exchanged to satisfy the [[project-information-requirements]] (PIR) and support key decision points.

## Alignment with Decision Gateways

```
[Information Exchange Milestone] ──► [Review Period (X weeks)] ──► [Key Decision Point / Gate]
```

- **Separation of Exchange and Decision**: An information exchange must take place *in advance* of the decision point to allow the appointing party sufficient time to perform QA/QC, audit compliance, and evaluate the information.
- **Multiple Milestones per Stage**: A single work stage (e.g. RIBA Stage 3 Spatial Coordination) often contains several intermediate milestones (e.g. Planning submission, client options review, regulatory check).
