# Information Model Review and Acceptance

## Core Concept
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.7, information model delivery is a formal two-step gateway:
1. **Delivery Team Authorization (§5.7.2)**: The [[lead-appointed-party]] reviews the federated information model against the MIDP and EIR, authorizing its submission.
2. **Appointing Party Acceptance (§5.7.4)**: The [[appointing-party]] reviews the submission strictly against pre-established acceptance criteria within agreed review windows.

## Review Criteria
- **Geometric Coordination**: Clash-free multidisciplinary federation.
- **Alphanumeric Completeness**: COBie and attribute completeness matching LOIN specifications.
- **Deliverable Conformity**: Verification against all scheduled MIDP container requirements.
