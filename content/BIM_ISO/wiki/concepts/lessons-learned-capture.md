# Lessons Learned Capture

## Core Concept
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.8.2, capturing lessons learned at project completion ensures that insights regarding people, process, technology, and data are systematically embedded into future project requirements and delivery workflows.

## Implementation Framework
1. **Multidisciplinary Workshop**: Debrief sessions involving client, designers, and contractors.
2. **Thematic Review**: Analyzing performance across Information Standards, CDE tools, LOIN clarity, and delivery timelines.
3. **Standard Updating**: Updating corporate templates, family libraries, and BEP standards for future tenders.
