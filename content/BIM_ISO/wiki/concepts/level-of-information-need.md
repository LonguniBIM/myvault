# Level of Information Need

The **Level of Information Need** is a framework defined in ISO 19650 that establishes the extent and granularity of information required at different stages of a project.

It encompasses how much detail is required for different components of the [[information-model]], including:
- **Geometric detail**: The geometric representation of the object or information model.
- **Non-Geometric data**: Defines how much alphanumeric data/meta-data is required to be provided at different stages of the project.
