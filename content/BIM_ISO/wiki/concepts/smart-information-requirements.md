# SMART Information Requirements

To prevent ambiguity, excessive cost, or delivery failures, information requirements in the [[exchange-information-requirements]] (EIR) should be formulated and audited against the **SMART** criteria:

| Letter | Attribute | ISO 19650 Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S** | **Specific** | Clearly identifies the exact information purpose (e.g. FM maintenance, clash avoidance). Informed by OIR and AIR. |
| **M** | **Measurable** | Quantifiable deliverables and testable questions (e.g., PIR questions that can be verified against geometric models or schedules). |
| **A** | **Achievable** | Demands deliverables feasible within current technology and supply chain maturity; avoids blanket data dumps. |
| **R** | **Realistic** | Balanced in terms of volume and cost; e.g., avoids asking for "all COBie fields" indiscriminately. |
| **T** | **Timely** | Explicitly linked to work stages, key decision points, and delivery milestones, with built-in client review periods. |
