# Tender Evaluation Criteria (ISO 19650)

Under ISO 19650-2 §5.2.3, the [[appointing-party]] must establish clear, objective evaluation criteria within the [[invitation-to-tender]] (ITT) to determine whether tendering delivery teams are capable of meeting the project's Exchange Information Requirements (EIR).

## Core Evaluation Categories & Mechanisms

ISO 19650 and industry best practice group tender evaluation considerations into the following distinct sections:

| Evaluation Consideration | Supporting System / Mechanism | Focus / Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | **Project Procurement** *(Commercial)* | Commercial evaluation of works/services. *Note: Price is excluded from the IM technical evaluation; information procurement must be integrated into overall project procurement.* |
| **Understanding of Requirements** | [[pre-appointment-bep]] | Evaluates how thoroughly the delivery team grasped the EIR, LOIN requirements, and project information objectives. |
| **Compliance with ITT** | **Completed Tender Return / Checklist** | Verifies complete, compliant responses to all mandatory questions and submission requirements. |
| **Technical Skills & Resource Availability** | **Delivery Team Capability & Capacity Summary** | Quantifies available personnel, qualifications, software competencies, hardware capacity, and workload bandwidth. |
| **Management Skills & Systems** | **BEP & Information Delivery Risk Assessment** | Assesses governance structure, CDE management procedures, QA/QC controls, and mitigation of identified information risks. |
| **Proposed Methodology** | **Mobilization Plan** | Examines concrete plans to test proposals, configure software, train staff, and validate interoperability prior to production. |
| **Information Management Competency** | **IM Competency Assessment** | Audits dedicated knowledge and past experience of individuals assigned to the Information Management function. |

## Critical Principle: Price vs. Information Management

> **Key Rule**: Price is **not** part of the Information Management portion of ISO 19650. 
Information management focuses on capability, capacity, quality assurance, and methodology. Price considerations belong to commercial project procurement. Conflating the two often leads to selecting teams with inadequate BIM capabilities simply because their base commercial bid was lowest.
