# Tender Response Requirements (ISO 19650)

Under ISO 19650-2 §5.2.3 and §5.3, the [[appointing-party]] establishes mandatory submission deliverables that tendering organizations must furnish within their tender return. 

## The 6 Core Systems and Submission Artifacts

To enable systematic and rigorous technical evaluation, tender responses require six supporting submission artifacts:

```
Tender Response Submission
├── 1. Project Procurement (Commercial Return)
├── 2. Pre-appointment BIM Execution Plan (BEP)
├── 3. Information Management Competency Assessments
├── 4. Delivery Team Capability and Capacity Summary
├── 5. Information Delivery Risk Assessment (Risk Register)
└── 6. Mobilization Plan
```

### 1. Project Procurement Return
- Covers commercial bids, fees, insurances, and contractual commercial commitments. Evaluated by project commercial teams, not the IM evaluation panel.

### 2. Pre-appointment BIM Execution Plan (BEP)
- Details the delivery team’s proposed information standards, production methods, procedures, and collaborative modeling workflows.
- Serves as direct evidence of understanding the EIR.

### 3. Information Management Competency Assessments
- Explicit documentation of the qualifications, project experience, and training of personnel nominated for the information management function.

### 4. Delivery Team Capability and Capacity Summary
- Assessment of task team resource availability, IT infrastructure, software licenses, and team bandwidth during the project lifecycle.

### 5. Information Delivery Risk Assessment (Information Risk Register)
- Identification of specific technical, organizational, or communication risks associated with information delivery, along with proposed mitigations.

### 6. Mobilization Plan
- Practical roadmap detailing how the prospective team will test its proposals, establish software interoperability, configure CDE workflows, and test data exchanges *before* live information production commences.
