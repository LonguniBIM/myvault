# UK BIM Maturity Stages (Bew-Richards to ISO 19650)

The **BIM Maturity Model** (originally developed by Mark Bew and Mervyn Richards as the "BIM Wedge") defines the evolution of digital collaboration from uncoordinated CAD to integrated cloud ecosystems.

## The Evolution: Levels to Stages

### Legacy UK BIM Levels (PAS 1192)
- **Level 0**: Unmanaged 2D CAD with paper or unstructured electronic exchange.
- **Level 1**: Managed 2D/3D CAD using basic CDE and standard naming conventions (BS 1192:2007).
- **Level 2**: Collaborative BIM where separate discipline models are developed in 3D and federated within a CDE using structured data (EIR, BEP, COBie). Centrally mandated in the UK from April 2016.
- **Level 3**: Integrated lifecycle models hosted on centralized cloud servers (open data models / iBIM).

### ISO 19650 Maturity Stages
With the release of ISO 19650, static numbered levels were replaced by **Stages of Maturity** defined by minimum functional criteria:
- **Stage 1**: Standardized 2D/3D drawings with basic CDE container management.
- **Stage 2**: Collaborative information production according to ISO 19650-1 and 19650-2 with federated models and structured information requirements.
- **Stage 3**: Enterprise-wide digital twins, integrated asset databases, and cloud-native lifecycle services.
