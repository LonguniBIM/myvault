# Appointed Party

## Definition
Under ISO 19650-1 clause 3.2.4, an **Appointed Party** is any provider of services, materials, or products to an appointment. Within a [[delivery-team]], appointed parties represent discipline task teams (e.g. architects, structural engineers, MEP engineers, specialist sub-contractors, fabricators) reporting to the [[lead-appointed-party]] or directly to the [[appointing-party]].

## Key Responsibilities
1. **Capability & Capacity Declaration (§5.3.3)**: Provide evidence of skills, software competencies, hardware capacity, and availability.
2. **Establish Task Information Delivery Plan (§5.4.4)**: Author and maintain a dedicated [[task-information-delivery-plan]] (TIDP) scheduling all container deliverables.
3. **Generate Information Containers (§5.6.1)**: Author geometric, alphanumeric, and documentation containers strictly within the CDE Work In Progress (WIP) state.
4. **Undertake Internal QA Checks (§5.6.2)**: Execute technical, dimensional, LOIN, and standards compliance checks before sharing.
5. **Participate in Interdisciplinary Coordination (§5.6.4)**: Resolve clashes and verify spatial federation with other task teams in CDE Shared state.
