# Appointing Party

The **Appointing Party** (defined in ISO 19650-1 §3.2.3 and ISO 19650-2) is the receiver of information concerning works, goods, or services from a lead appointed party. Typically, the appointing party initiates the project, funds the project, or approves the project brief (historically referred to as the Client, Employer, or Asset Owner).

## Role in the Invitation to Tender Stage (ISO 19650-2 §5.2)

During the tender stage, the Appointing Party is responsible for establishing the framework against which prospective delivery teams are procured and assessed:

1. **Establish Exchange Information Requirements (EIR)** (§5.2.1):
   - Derives project-specific requirements from the Organizational Information Requirements (OIR), Asset Information Requirements (AIR), and Project Information Requirements (PIR).
   - Establishes the Level of Information Need (LOIN) framework.
   - Defines acceptance criteria governed by project standards, methods, procedures, and shared resources.

2. **Assemble Reference Information and Shared Resources** (§5.2.2):
   - Provides existing asset information, BIM models, surveys, and templates (e.g., BEP templates, responsibility matrices) to avoid duplicated effort and facilitate comparison.

3. **Establish Tender Response Requirements & Evaluation Criteria** (§5.2.3):
   - Specifies the required tender returns (Pre-appointment BEP, competency assessments, capability/capacity summary, risk assessment, mobilization plan).
   - Formulates clear criteria for accepting or rejecting proposed information management approaches.

4. **Compile ITT Information & Publish to CDE** (§5.2.4):
   - Assembles the complete ITT package.
   - Assigns unambiguous CDE status codes defining suitability for use before sharing on the project Common Data Environment.
