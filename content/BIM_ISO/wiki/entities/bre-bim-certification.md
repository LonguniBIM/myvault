# BRE BIM Certification

## Definition
**BRE BIM Certification** represents independent third-party professional accreditation and business certification schemes administered by the Building Research Establishment (BRE Academy). It provides verified evidence of compliance with the ISO 19650 series and UK BIM Framework standards.

## Certification Streams
1. **Individual Certification**:
   - *BIM Certified Practitioner*: Validates knowledge of ISO 19650 concepts, roles, and project delivery principles.
   - *BIM Certified Professional*: Advanced qualification confirming practical project information management competence.
2. **Business Certification**:
   - Assesses corporate policies, BIM execution procedures, software capabilities, and live project deliverables.
   - Requires annual surveillance audits to ensure ongoing compliance with ISO 19650-2 delivery requirements.

## Procurement Impact
Certified businesses and practitioners gain competitive pre-qualification advantages, satisfying tender evaluation criteria without requiring extensive bespoke capability re-assessments.
