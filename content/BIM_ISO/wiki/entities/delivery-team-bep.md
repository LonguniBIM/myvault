# Delivery Team BIM Execution Plan (Confirmed BEP)

## Definition
The **Delivery Team BEP (Post-Appointment Confirmed BEP)** is the ratified management and technical document established under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.4.1. It converts the provisional commitments made in the [[pre-appointment-bep]] into a legally binding, operational baseline for the entire [[delivery-team]].

## Core Sections & Components
1. **Management**: Named individuals undertaking IM functions, decision-making escalation protocols, and meeting rhythms.
2. **Planning & Documentation**: Finalized milestones aligned with the [[master-information-delivery-plan]] and [[detailed-responsibility-matrix]].
3. **Federation Strategy**: Subdivision of information models into distinct containers, spatial coordination origin (easting, northing, elevation), and orientation.
4. **Information Standard Compliance**: Exact software versions, IFC export configurations, Uniclass classification rules, and container naming syntax.
5. **IT & CDE Protocols**: CDE folder structures, access security matrices, and automated validation rules.

## Standard References
