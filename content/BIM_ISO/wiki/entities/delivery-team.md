# Delivery Team

## Definition
Under ISO 19650-1 clause 3.2.6, a **Delivery Team** comprises the [[lead-appointed-party]] and their associated internal and external [[appointed-party]] members (task teams). A project typically features multiple delivery teams (e.g., Design Delivery Team, Main Construction Delivery Team, Specialist Commissioning Delivery Team), each governed by its own lead appointment.

## Structure & Dynamics
- **Leadership**: Coordinated by the [[lead-appointed-party]].
- **Composition**: Multiple task teams generating specialized discipline containers.
- **Operational Alignment**: Bound collectively by the confirmed [[delivery-team-bep]] and the [[master-information-delivery-plan]].
