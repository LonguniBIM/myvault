# Exchange Information Requirements (EIR)

The **Exchange Information Requirements (EIR)** (ISO 19650-1 §3.3.4, ISO 19650-2 §5.2.1) is a structured specification establishing the information that must be delivered by a lead appointed party to an [[appointing-party]] at specific information exchange milestones.

## Role in ISO 19650 Information Hierarchy
The EIR sits at the operational core of information procurement:
```
Organizational Information Requirements (OIR)
         │
         ├── Project Information Requirements (PIR) ──► Exchange Information Requirements (EIR)
         │                                                      │
Asset Information Requirements (AIR) ───────────────────────────┘
```

- **Informed by**: High-level strategic drivers (OIR), asset management needs (AIR), and project decision milestones (PIR).
- **Cascaded**: Across a project, every appointment (at client level or tiered supply chain level) must include its corresponding EIR.

## Key Content per ISO 19650-2 §5.2.1
1. **Information Purposes**: Why the information is needed (e.g., statutory approval, cost checking, FM operation).
2. **Level of Information Need (LOIN)**: Extent and granularity of geometrical, alphanumeric, and document data.
3. **Acceptance Criteria**: Defined rules governed by the Project Information Standard and Production Methods & Procedures.
4. **Milestone Dates & Review Timescales**: Clear deadlines incorporating adequate time for client internal review and assurance.
