# Information Management Function

Under **ISO 19650-2 Clause 5.1.1**, the Information Management Function refers to the comprehensive suite of ~40 distinct tasks and activities required to manage information across the asset delivery lifecycle.

## The Three Function Types

ISO 19650 identifies three primary functions:
1. **Asset Information Management Function**: Operational phase tasks relating to maintaining and updating the Asset Information Model (AIM).
2. **Project Information Management Function**: Delivery phase governance tasks ensuring project-wide compliance with standards, CDE protocols, and milestones.
3. **Task Information Management Function**: Production-level tasks executed by individual task teams generating specific geometrical models, data schedules, and documentation.

## Key Principles
- **Function vs. Job Title**: The standard defines functions, not individuals or job titles. An appointing party may assign functions to an internal resource, split them across multiple individuals, or appoint a specialist third-party consultant.
- **Competency, Capability & Capacity**: Whomever is assigned must possess verified competency (qualifications/experience), capability (tools/methods), and capacity (resource availability).
- **Residual Accountability**: The Appointing Party remains ultimately accountable for information governance even when delegating tasks.
