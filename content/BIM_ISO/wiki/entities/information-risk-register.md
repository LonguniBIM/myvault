# Information Risk Register

## Definition
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.3.5, the **Information Risk Register** is a specialized risk management artifact compiled by the prospective [[lead-appointed-party]]. It identifies specific risks associated with information management, model coordination, software interoperability, and delivery timelines, establishing proactive mitigations.

## Standard Risk Categories
- **Interoperability & Software Risks**: File format incompatibilities, schema mapping errors, software version discrepancies.
- **Resource & Capability Risks**: Skill gaps in supply chain, bandwidth bottlenecks, staffing turnover.
- **Process & Coordination Risks**: Uncoordinated spatial origins, scope gaps in responsibility matrix, delayed review turnaround.
- **Security & Data Loss Risks**: Unsecured file sharing, corruption of centralized CDE repositories, confidentiality breaches.
