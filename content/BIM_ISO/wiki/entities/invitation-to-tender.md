# Invitation to Tender (ITT)

The **Invitation to Tender (ITT)** package under ISO 19650-2 §5.2.4 is the compilation of procurement and information management documents assembled by the [[appointing-party]] to invite prospective [[lead-appointed-party]] organizations to bid for an appointment.

## Core Information Management Components

Under ISO 19650-2, the ITT package incorporates critical information management deliverables alongside traditional commercial documents:

1. **Exchange Information Requirements (EIR)** (ISO 19650-2 §5.2.1):
   - Details specific information deliverables, LOIN, milestone dates, and acceptance criteria.
2. **Reference Information & Shared Resources** (ISO 19650-2 §5.2.2):
   - Existing site/building models, utility surveys, BEP templates, responsibility matrix templates.
3. **Tender Response Requirements & Evaluation Criteria** (ISO 19650-2 §5.2.3):
   - Clear requirements for what tendering delivery teams must submit (see [[tender-response-requirements]]) and how proposals will be evaluated (see [[tender-evaluation-criteria]]).

## Common Data Environment (CDE) Protocol in ITT

A mandatory requirement of ISO 19650-2 §5.2.4:
> The appointing party shall ensure that each information container within the ITT package is assigned a **status code** defining the correct use before sharing on the project CDE.

- **Status Code Requirement**: Containers distributed to tenderers must clearly indicate their permitted use (e.g., tender reference, informational, suitable for pricing).
- Prevents ambiguity regarding whether shared resources (such as existing condition models) can be contractually relied upon.
