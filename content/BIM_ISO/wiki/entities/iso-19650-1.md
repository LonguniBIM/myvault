# ISO 19650-1 (Concepts and Principles)

**ISO 19650-1:2018** (*Organization and digitization of information about buildings and civil engineering works, including building information modelling (BIM) — Information management using building information modelling — Part 1: Concepts and principles*) establishes the foundational concepts and international principles for managing information throughout the lifecycle of built assets.

## Core Concepts Introduced
1. **Information Hierarchy**: Cascading relationship between OIR, PIR, AIR, and EIR.
2. **Common Data Environment (CDE)**: Information container states (WIP, Shared, Published, Archive) and gate workflows.
3. **Information Model**: Differentiation between Project Information Model (PIM) and Asset Information Model (AIM).
4. **Level of Information Need**: Framework defining granularity for geometrical, alphanumeric, and documentary requirements.
5. **Information Functions**: Separation of Asset, Project, and Task information management functions.
