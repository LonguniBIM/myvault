# ISO 19650-2 (Delivery Phase of Assets)

**ISO 19650-2:2018** (*Part 2: Delivery phase of the assets*) specifies the mandatory information management process comprising 8 discrete activity stages during design, procurement, and construction:

```
1. Assessment and Need (§5.1)
2. Invitation to Tender (§5.2)
3. Tender Response (§5.3)
4. Appointment (§5.4)
5. Mobilization (§5.5)
6. Collaborative Production of Information (§5.6)
7. Information Model Delivery (§5.7)
8. Project Closeout (§5.8)
```

## Key Deliverables
- PIR, Project Information Standard, Methods & Procedures, CDE Protocol (§5.1).
- EIR, Reference Information, Shared Resources, Tender Criteria (§5.2).
- Pre-appointment BEP, Competency Assessments, Risk Register, Mobilization Plan (§5.3).
- Confirmed BEP, TIDPs, MIDP (§5.4).
