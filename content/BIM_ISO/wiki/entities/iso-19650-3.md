# ISO 19650-3 (Operational Phase of Assets)

**ISO 19650-3:2020** (*Part 3: Operational phase of the assets*) specifies information management processes during the lifetime operation and maintenance of built assets, replacing PAS 1192-3.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Asset Information Model (AIM)**: Digital repository tracking maintainable assets, O&M data, and spatial performance.
- **Asset Information Requirements (AIR)**: Operational data needs informing facilities management software (CAFM / IWMS).
- **Trigger Events**: Event-driven management separating planned triggers (routine inspections) from unforeseen triggers (equipment failure, emergency repairs).
