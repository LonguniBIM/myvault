# ISO 19650-4 (Information Exchange)

**ISO 19650-4:2022** (*Part 4: Information exchange*) establishes concepts and criteria for executing, validating, and verifying individual information exchanges, transitioning from the UK's BS 1192-4 (COBie).

## Core Principles
- **Definition of Exchange**: An act of satisfying an information requirement or part thereof.
- **Verification & Validation**: Systematic criteria ensuring data drops meet specified schemas, classification syntax (Uniclass 2015), and quality rules before acceptance.
- **Open Standards Support**: Mandating interoperable OpenBIM schemas (IFC, COBie, BCF) to prevent proprietary lock-in.
