# Lead Appointed Party EIR

## Definition
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.4.3, the **Lead Appointed Party EIR (Cascaded EIR)** represents the filtered, discipline-specific information requirements established by the [[lead-appointed-party]] for each sub-contracted [[appointed-party]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Cascaded Scope**: Translates client-level requirements into clear technical instructions for specific trade or design scopes.
- **Interim Delivery Gates**: Schedules sub-contractor delivery milestones ahead of client milestones to allow lead coordination and review.
- **Contractual Integration**: Attached directly to sub-appointment contracts (§5.4.6).
