# Lead Appointed Party

## Definition
Under ISO 19650-1 clause 3.2.3 and ISO 19650-2 clause 5.3/5.4, the **Lead Appointed Party (LAP)** is the entity responsible for coordinating information exchange and delivery across a specific [[delivery-team]]. On a typical capital project, lead appointed parties are principal designers, lead engineering consultants, or main contractors appointed directly by the [[appointing-party]].

## Key Responsibilities
1. **Undertake or Delegate IM Function (§5.3.1)**: Nominate competent individuals to manage information workflows and establish probity arrangements.
2. **Establish the Pre-Appointment BEP (§5.3.2)**: Coordinate prospective task teams and articulate delivery strategy during tender.
3. **Assess Supply Chain Capability & Capacity (§5.3.3)**: Verify competence, IT resources, and bandwidth of all prospective [[appointed-party]] members.
4. **Confirm the Delivery Team BEP (§5.4.1)**: Finalize post-appointment execution plans, federation strategies, and spatial container breakdown.
5. **Establish Detailed Responsibility Matrix (§5.4.2)**: Assign information containers and Level of Information Need to task teams.
6. **Establish Lead Appointed Party EIR (§5.4.3)**: Cascade appointing party information requirements down to sub-contracted task teams.
7. **Aggregate the MIDP (§5.4.5)**: Compile all individual [[task-information-delivery-plan]] (TIDPs) into a unified [[master-information-delivery-plan]].
8. **Authorize Information Delivery (§5.7.2)**: Review and approve multidisciplinary information models before submission to the client.
