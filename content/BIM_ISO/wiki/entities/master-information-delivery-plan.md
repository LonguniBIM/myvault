# Master Information Delivery Plan (MIDP)

## Definition
Under ISO 19650-2 clause 5.4.5, the **Master Information Delivery Plan (MIDP)** is the primary information production schedule for the entire [[delivery-team]]. It aggregates and harmonizes all individual [[task-information-delivery-plan]] (TIDPs) into a unified master timeline.

## Governance & Functions
- **Aggregation**: Compiled and managed by the [[lead-appointed-party]].
- **Milestone Alignment**: Directly aligned with client Information Delivery Milestones (§5.1.3).
- **Critical Path Tracking**: Identifies cross-team information dependencies and potential schedule gridlocks.
- **Contractual Baseline**: Attached to appointment documents (§5.4.6) as the baseline for earned progress verification.

## Standard References
- ISO 19650-2:2018 §5.4.5 (Establish the master information delivery plan)
