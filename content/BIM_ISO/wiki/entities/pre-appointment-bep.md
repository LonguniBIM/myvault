# Pre-appointment BIM Execution Plan (BEP)

The **Pre-appointment BIM Execution Plan (BEP)** (ISO 19650-2 §5.2.3 & §5.3.2) is a prospective delivery team's proposed strategy and approach submitted as part of its tender return, demonstrating how it intends to satisfy the [[appointing-party]]'s Exchange Information Requirements (EIR).

## Purpose in Tender Evaluation

Unlike the post-appointment BEP (which is refined and agreed upon after contract award as a living delivery tool), the Pre-appointment BEP is primarily an **evaluative instrument**:
- Demonstrates the prospective delivery team's **understanding of the requirements**.
- Outlines the proposed management skills, collaboration systems, software, and delivery methods.
- Details the delivery team's organizational structure and technical capabilities.

## Key Content Sections
A robust pre-appointment BEP typically includes:
1. **Delivery Team Structure**: Key roles, information management function leads, and reporting lines.
2. **Proposed Information Standards & Methods**: Confirmation of alignment with project information standards and production procedures.
3. **Proposed Schedule & Milestones**: High-level alignment with delivery milestones and information exchanges.
4. **Information Delivery Strategy**: Approach to collaborative modeling, container division, and clash detection.
5. **Software & Technology Stack**: Versions, exchange formats (IFC, BCF), and compatibility tests.

## Relationship to Post-Appointment BEP
- Following contract award, the successful lead appointed party develops this preliminary plan into the formal **Post-appointment BEP** (ISO 19650-2 §5.4.1), incorporating detailed TIDPs (Task Information Delivery Plans) and the consolidated MIDP (Master Information Delivery Plan).
