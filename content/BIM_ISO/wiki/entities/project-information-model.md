# Project Information Model (PIM)

## Definition
Under ISO 19650-1 clause 3.3.10 and ISO 19650-2 clause 5.7/5.8, the **Project Information Model (PIM)** is the structured information model delivered during the capital design and construction phases of an asset. It comprises federated 3D geometric models, non-geometric alphanumeric databases (such as COBie), and associated documentation containers.

## Lifecycle Transition
- **Authoring & Delivery**: Progressively developed by the [[delivery-team]] during design and construction.
- **Verification Gate**: Formally authorized by the [[lead-appointed-party]] and accepted by the [[appointing-party]] at milestone deliveries (§5.7).
- **Project Close-out**: At handover, the PIM is preserved in the CDE Archive (§5.8.1), while maintainable asset data is filtered and extracted into the **Asset Information Model (AIM)** under ISO 19650-3.
