# Project Information Production Methods and Procedures

The **Project Information Production Methods and Procedures** (ISO 19650-2 §5.1.5) defines the operational "how" of information generation, checking, coordination, and delivery.

## Key Process Coverage
1. **Capture of Existing Information**: Specific procedures for executing measured surveys, point clouds, and condition assessments.
2. **Generation of New Information**: Use of verified templates, software versions, and geometric modeling tolerances.
3. **Internal Review & Approval Gateways**: Explicit QA/QC steps and author sign-off required prior to sharing models on the CDE.
4. **Security & Sensitive Information Handling**: Protocols for encrypting or restricting access to sensitive assets.
5. **Delivery Configuration**: Exact methods and automated scripts for packaging and transmitting information to the appointing party.
