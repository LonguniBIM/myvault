# Project Information Requirements (PIR)

The **Project Information Requirements (PIR)** (ISO 19650-1 §3.3.3, ISO 19650-2 §5.1.2) explain the information required by the [[appointing-party]] to answer strategic questions and make informed decisions at key project gateways.

## Purpose & Scope
PIR are expressed as **high-level, non-technical questions** formulated by the appointing party. Typical decision gateways include:
- *Strategic Definition*: Does the business case provide sufficient justification to proceed?
- *Design Concept*: Does the proposed massing comply with zoning regulations and sustainability targets?
- *Technical Design*: Does the coordinated model meet the federation strategy without unresolvable clashes?
- *Tender Award*: Does the preferred bidder demonstrate verified information management capability?

## Hierarchy Relationship
```
Organizational Information Requirements (OIR)
         │
         ├── Project Information Requirements (PIR) ──► Exchange Information Requirements (EIR)
```
- PIR focuses strictly on the decision-making needs of the Appointing Party during capital delivery.
- Answers to PIR questions are delivered via information deliverables defined in the EIR.
