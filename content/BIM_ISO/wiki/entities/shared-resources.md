# Shared Resources & Reference Information

Under **ISO 19650-2 §5.1.6 & §5.2.2**, the [[appointing-party]] must assemble and provide reference information and shared resources to prospective delivery teams to facilitate consistency, eliminate duplication, and streamline evaluation.

## Classification of Provided Assets

1. **Reference Information**:
   - Existing asset models and 2D CAD surveys.
   - Ground investigation reports, utility surveys, environmental data.
   - Historical maintenance logs.

2. **Shared Resources**:
   - Standardized [[pre-appointment-bep]] templates directly mapped to the EIR.
   - Responsibility matrix (RACI) templates.
   - Information container naming and classification guides.
   - CAD / BIM object libraries and template files.

## Benefits
- **Direct Correlation**: Ensures responses from different bidders align 1-to-1 with EIR questions.
- **Automated Comparison**: Enables programmatic or standardized comparative scoring.
- **Waste Reduction**: Prevents bidders from re-creating standard templates from scratch.
