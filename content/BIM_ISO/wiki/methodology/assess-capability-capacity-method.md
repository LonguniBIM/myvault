# Assess Delivery Team Capability & Capacity Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for evaluating prospective task teams to ensure competence, resource capacity, and IT readiness before tender submission (ISO 19650-2 §5.3.3).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Issue Standardized Assessment Questionnaires
Issue capability and capacity assessment forms (aligned with PAS 91 / CPIx) to all internal teams and prospective sub-consultants.

### Step 2: Evaluate Technical Track Record & Certifications
Verify past project case studies, client references, and staff certifications (e.g. BRE BIM Certification).

### Step 3: Audit Hardware, Bandwidth & Software Licenses
Verify that proposed task teams possess compatible workstations, certified graphics hardware, reliable broadband, and valid software subscriptions.

### Step 4: Assess Resource Loading & Milestone Availability
Map team staffing rosters against project information delivery milestones to identify over-allocation risks.

### Step 5: Identify Gaps and Formulate Training Plans
Highlight capability deficiencies and establish targeted upskilling modules to be executed during the mobilization phase.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: Every appointed task team provides verified evidence of software competency and signed resource commitment.
- **Fail**: Supply chain selected solely on price without verifying ability to author compliant IFC/COBie deliverables.
