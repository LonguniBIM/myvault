# Assign Information Management Function Method

This methodology operationalizes **ISO 19650-2 Clause 5.1.1** for assigning information management responsibilities from project inception.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Identify Required IM Tasks**: Review the 40 standard delivery-phase activities defined in ISO 19650-2.
2. **Determine Allocation Model**: Evaluate whether the appointing party possesses internal capability or requires a third-party consultant appointment.
3. **Draft Scope of Services**: Define clear requirements for competency, tools, and staffing bandwidth.
4. **Publish Responsibility Matrix**: Record exact role allocations using a RACI matrix appended to contracts.
