# Capture Lessons Learned Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for capturing, documenting, and disseminating project lessons learned to drive organizational continuous improvement (ISO 19650-2 §5.8.2).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Distribute Post-Project Debrief Surveys
Issue structured feedback questionnaires to client, lead appointed parties, and appointed task teams.

### Step 2: Convene Multidisciplinary Close-Out Workshop
Facilitate a collaborative review session analyzing performance across People, Process, Technology, and Data.

### Step 3: Evaluate Performance Against BEP & EIR Baselines
Review metrics: delivery milestone timeliness, clash resolution efficiency, and CDE container rejection rates.

### Step 4: Compile the Lessons Learned Register
Document recurring friction points, successful innovations, and specific procedural recommendations.

### Step 5: Update Corporate Templates & Standards
Feed verified lessons into corporate BEP templates, family libraries, and future tender evaluation guidelines.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: Concrete procedural improvements implemented into corporate standards for future projects.
- **Fail**: Workshop treated as a fault-finding exercise or report filed away without updating working templates.
