# Complete Appointment Documents Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure to assemble, review, and legally execute all ISO 19650 information management annexes in project contracts (ISO 19650-2 §5.4.6).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Compile Lead Appointed Party Annex Bundle
Assemble the mandatory 6 annexes: EIR, Information Standard, Production Methods & Procedures, Information Protocol, confirmed BEP, and MIDP.

### Step 2: Compile Sub-Appointed Party Annex Bundles
Assemble corresponding packages for each sub-appointment: Cascaded EIR, Information Protocol, relevant TIDP, and confirmed BEP.

### Step 3: Perform Legal Precedence & Cross-Reference Audit
Review contract clauses to confirm that the Information Protocol takes precedence over standard commercial terms in technical data matters.

### Step 4: Secure Multi-Tier Signatures
Execute formal contractual agreements between Appointing Party and Lead Appointed Parties, and cascaded sub-contracts down the supply chain.

### Step 5: Archive Executed Contracts in CDE
Deposit signed contract annexes into the secure, restricted area of the CDE as the immutable legal baseline.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: All 6 information management annexes attached and cross-referenced in executed agreements.
- **Fail**: Contracts signed without attaching the confirmed BEP or MIDP, leaving requirements non-binding.
