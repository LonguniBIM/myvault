# Confirm Delivery Team BEP Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure to finalize and ratify the Post-Appointment Confirmed BEP upon contract award (ISO 19650-2 §5.4.1).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Update Pre-Appointment Commitments
Review the Pre-BEP against the final contract scope and client clarifications, updating named individuals and schedules.

### Step 2: Finalize Federation & Coordinate Strategy
Lock in project origin coordinates, true north orientation, level datum, spatial boundary limits, and container volume limits.

### Step 3: Formalize Container Naming Standards
Confirm container naming convention syntax, field tokens, discipline codes, and revision conventions per project standards.

### Step 4: Secure Supply Chain Ratification
Distribute the updated BEP to all appointed task teams for formal review, resolving comments and obtaining written sign-off.

### Step 5: Submit Confirmed BEP to Client
Submit the finalized Delivery Team BEP via the CDE for formal client approval and contractual archiving.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: 100% of task team leads formally ratify the BEP; coordinate origins verified with surveyor datum.
- **Fail**: Discrepancies between confirmed BEP software versions and actual tools installed across task teams.
