# Deliver Information Model Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for the lead appointed party to review, authorize, and submit the Project Information Model for client milestone acceptance (ISO 19650-2 §5.7).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Collate Milestone Deliverables per MIDP
Filter all Published and authorized containers required for the designated Information Delivery Milestone.

### Step 2: Conduct Lead Party Federation & Compliance Audit
Perform final interdisciplinary federation check, verify clash clearance, and validate data completeness (COBie audit).

### Step 3: Authorize Information Model Submission
Lead Appointed Party IM formalizes the submission authorization certificate within the CDE.

### Step 4: Issue Submission to Appointing Party
Transmit the Information Model package to the client in the CDE with delivery notice and compliance report.

### Step 5: Track Client Review & Formal Acceptance
Monitor appointing party review timescales; receive formal Acceptance Notice or detailed Non-Conformance Schedule.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: Delivery package contains 100% of containers scheduled in MIDP; zero unresolved critical clashes.
- **Fail**: Model delivered with missing discipline containers or rejected by client due to non-compliant attributes.
