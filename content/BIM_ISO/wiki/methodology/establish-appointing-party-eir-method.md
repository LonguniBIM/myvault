# Establish Appointing Party EIR Method

This methodology operationalizes **ISO 19650-2 Clause 5.2.1** for the [[appointing-party]] to systematically establish its Exchange Information Requirements (EIR).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Synthesize High-Level Requirements
- Review OIR (organizational needs), AIR (operational asset data), and PIR (project decision questions).
- Filter out irrelevant organizational items to retain only requirements relevant to this specific appointment.

### Step 2: Formulate Specific Information Purposes
- Define why information is required at each key milestone.
- Apply [[smart-information-requirements]] criteria to ensure each requirement is measurable and realistic.

### Step 3: Establish Level of Information Need (LOIN) Framework
- Specify the extent and granularity of geometrical, alphanumeric, and documentation requirements.
- Adopt standard classification (e.g. Uniclass 2015).

### Step 4: Define Acceptance Criteria & Governing Rules
- Reference the Project Information Standard and Project Information Production Methods & Procedures.
- Document clear pass/fail criteria for container verification.

### Step 5: Establish Delivery Milestones & Assurance Windows
- Schedule information exchange dates aligned with project work stages.
- Explicitly reserve adequate review periods for internal client assurance.
