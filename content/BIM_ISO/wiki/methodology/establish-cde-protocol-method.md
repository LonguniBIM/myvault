# Establish CDE & Information Protocol Method

This methodology operationalizes **ISO 19650-2 Clauses 5.1.6, 5.1.7, and 5.1.8** for procuring, configuring, and contractually establishing the CDE solution and protocol.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Audit & Assemble Shared Resources (§5.1.6)**: Gather existing drawings, point clouds, and authoring templates into a centralized repository.
2. **Procure & Configure CDE Solution (§5.1.7)**: Set up the CDE technology platform with strict container naming validation, metadata attributes (status, revision, classification), and role-based permissions.
3. **Draft Project Information Protocol (§5.1.8)**: Establish legal clauses governing intellectual property, data reliance, status codes, and electronic exchange liabilities.
4. **Incorporate Protocol into Contract Packages**: Ensure the protocol is formally appended to all professional services and construction contracts.
