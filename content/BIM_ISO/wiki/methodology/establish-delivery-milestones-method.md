# Establish Information Delivery Milestones Method

This methodology operationalizes **ISO 19650-2 Clause 5.1.3** for creating a synchronized milestone schedule with review windows.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Define Plan of Work**: Adopt industry work stages (e.g., RIBA Plan of Work) and identify client-specific gates.
2. **Determine Decision Gateway Dates**: Set formal milestone dates for key employer approvals.
3. **Establish Information Exchange Dates**: Schedule delivery deadlines with an advance buffer (e.g. 2-4 weeks) prior to gates.
4. **Integrate into EIR & Contracts**: Embed the milestone schedule into the tender and appointment documentation.
