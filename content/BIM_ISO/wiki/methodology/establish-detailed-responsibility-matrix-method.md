# Establish Detailed Responsibility Matrix Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for allocating information container ownership, LOIN, and delivery milestones across appointed task teams (ISO 19650-2 §5.4.2).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Decompose Information Model Breakdown
Break down the project into discrete discipline information containers (architectural shells, structural frames, MEP systems).

### Step 2: Assign Task Team Ownership
Assign a single, unambiguous responsible authoring task team to each container.

### Step 3: Prescribe Level of Information Need (LOIN)
Specify geometric detail and alphanumeric property requirements for each container at each project milestone.

### Step 4: Assign RACI Governance
Define review, coordination, and authorization roles across contributing disciplines for each container.

### Step 5: Publish Baseline Matrix
Publish the agreed matrix to all task teams as the governing foundation for authoring their individual TIDPs.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: Zero unassigned or duplicate-authored containers; LOIN calibrated to milestone decision gates.
- **Fail**: Overlapping responsibilities where multiple disciplines author the same structural or MEP penetrations.
