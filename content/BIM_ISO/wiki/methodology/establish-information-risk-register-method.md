# Establish Information Risk Register Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for identifying, evaluating, and mitigating information management risks during tender preparation (ISO 19650-2 §5.3.5).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Identify Information Delivery Vulnerabilities
Brainstorm and record technical, organizational, and CDE risks (software incompatibilities, bandwidth limits, scope gaps).

### Step 2: Quantify Likelihood and Impact
Score each identified risk on a standard 5x5 matrix to calculate inherent risk ratings.

### Step 3: Formulate Proactive Mitigations
Establish practical technical mitigations (e.g. running IFC dry runs, standardizing coordinate seeds, provisioning backup CDE sync).

### Step 4: Assign Risk Owners
Assign every mitigation action to a named individual within the delivery team with defined completion deadlines.

### Step 5: Embed Register in Tender Return & Review Cycle
Submit the completed register with tender documents and schedule ongoing monthly project risk reviews.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: Register specifically addresses digital information exchange and interoperability risks.
- **Fail**: Register only restates generic health and safety risks without digital data considerations.
