# Establish Lead Appointed Party EIR Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for cascading appointing party requirements into tailored sub-contract information requirements (ISO 19650-2 §5.4.3).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Filter Appointing Party Requirements
Review the client EIR and isolate requirements relevant to specific sub-contracted disciplines.

### Step 2: Establish Discipline-Specific LOIN & Formats
Define exact model granularity, classification codes (Uniclass 2015), and file exchange formats (IFC, RVT, DWG).

### Step 3: Establish Cascaded Delivery Milestones
Set sub-contractor delivery dates with sufficient review buffers (typically 5-10 business days) prior to main client submission dates.

### Step 4: Define Sub-contract Quality Acceptance Criteria
Specify geometric tolerances, clash clearance thresholds, and QA check deliverables required upon submission.

### Step 5: Bind into Sub-appointment Contracts
Incorporate the cascaded EIR into tender documents and final sub-contract appointment packages.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: Cascaded EIR reflects all client mandates while filtering out irrelevant disciplines; milestone buffers included.
- **Fail**: Blanket copy-pasting of full client EIR without discipline customization or review buffers.
