# Establish Master Information Delivery Plan (MIDP) Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for aggregating, harmonizing, and baselining all task team TIDPs into the project MIDP (ISO 19650-2 §5.4.5).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Collect & Validate All Task Team TIDPs
Gather TIDPs from internal teams and sub-appointed task teams, auditing each for naming syntax and date formatting.

### Step 2: Merge Schedules & Map Inter-dependencies
Aggregate entries into a consolidated schedule; link preceding and succeeding containers to highlight critical paths.

### Step 3: Resolve Schedule Conflicts & Milestone Clashes
Identify circular dependencies or containers scheduled past client delivery milestones; negotiate adjustments with task teams.

### Step 4: Incorporate Review & Authorization Windows
Add lead party coordination windows (5 days) and client review windows (10-15 days) into the delivery timeline.

### Step 5: Baseline MIDP & Submit for Client Acceptance
Formally issue the baseline MIDP to the Appointing Party for formal contractual acceptance and configure CDE tracking.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: 100% of task team deliverables integrated; critical path visible; client review windows preserved.
- **Fail**: Deliverables scheduled on the client milestone date without lead review or coordination buffer.
