# Establish Pre-Appointment BEP Method

## Overview
A 6-step procedure for compiling the prospective delivery team's Pre-appointment BIM Execution Plan in direct response to the client EIR (ISO 19650-2 §5.3.2).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Map EIR Requirements to BEP Sections
Create a compliance matrix linking every client requirement in the EIR and Project Information Standard to a corresponding BEP section.

### Step 2: Formulate Proposed Federation Strategy
Define the spatial breakdown, discipline container division, volume boundaries, and coordinate management strategy.

### Step 3: Define IT Infrastructure & Toolset
Specify authoring software, point releases, open data schema workflows (IFC, BCF, COBie), and local CDE interface solutions.

### Step 4: Establish Delivery Team Structure
Document the delivery team organizational chart, task teams, nominated IM functions, and communication channels.

### Step 5: Draft High-Level Responsibility Matrix
Outline container authoring allocations and anticipated Level of Information Need at key delivery milestones.

### Step 6: Ratify and Publish Pre-BEP Submission
Assemble contributions from prospective task teams, conduct consistency reviews, and compile into the final tender submission.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: 100% of mandatory client EIR requirements explicitly addressed with technical methods.
- **Fail**: Generic template submitted with placeholder names, unaligned coordinates, or unverified software versions.
