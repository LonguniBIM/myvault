# Establish Production Methods and Procedures Method

This methodology operationalizes **ISO 19650-2 Clause 5.1.5** for drafting procedural workflows governing information production.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Define Survey & Existing Data Procedures**: Establish procedures for capturing point clouds and verifying historical records.
2. **Define Information Generation Protocols**: Specify authorized BIM templates, coordinates/datum setup, and modeling conventions.
3. **Map Internal Checking & Approval Workflows**: Detail mandatory QA/QC gates and sign-offs required prior to sharing.
4. **Establish Sensitive Asset Protocols**: Define security measures, access tiers, and redaction rules for confidential assets.
5. **Establish Delivery Configurations**: Specify packaging, container bundling, and transmission methods to the appointing party.
