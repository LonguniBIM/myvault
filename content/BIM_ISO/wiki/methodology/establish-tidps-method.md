# Establish Task Information Delivery Plans (TIDPs) Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for an appointed task team to author, schedule, and commit to its Task Information Delivery Plan (ISO 19650-2 §5.4.4).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Extract Container List from Responsibility Matrix
Identify all information containers allocated to the task team in the Detailed Responsibility Matrix.

### Step 2: Populate Container Metadata & Standard Identifiers
Assign project-compliant container IDs, descriptive titles, file formats, and required Level of Information Need.

### Step 3: Estimate Production Durations & Allocate Resources
Calculate authoring hours, allocate specific modelers/engineers, and set realistic production start and completion dates.

### Step 4: Identify Cross-Discipline Dependencies
Document preceding information containers required from other disciplines (e.g., structural grids required before facade modeling).

### Step 5: Submit TIDP for Lead Party Integration
Formally submit the ratified TIDP to the Lead Appointed Party for compilation into the Master Information Delivery Plan.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: All containers have defined authors, dates, LOIN, and explicit cross-discipline dependencies.
- **Fail**: Arbitrary completion dates set without consulting production staff or accounting for external dependencies.
