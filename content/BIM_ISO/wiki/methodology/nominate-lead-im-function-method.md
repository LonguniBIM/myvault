# Nominate Lead Information Management Function Method

## Overview
A 5-step procedure for a prospective lead appointed party to nominate competent individuals to undertake information management functions during tender response (ISO 19650-2 §5.3.1).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Review Client EIR & Scope Requirements
Analyze the Appointing Party's EIR, Project Information Standard, and project scale to quantify the required information management activities.

### Step 2: Establish Authority & Delegation Boundaries
Determine whether IM activities will be performed directly by internal staff or delegated to an external specialist IM consultant. Draft the formal IM Scope of Services.

### Step 3: Assess Candidate Competencies & Capacity
Review qualifications, BRE/professional certifications, software proficiency, and dedicated time capacity of nominated individuals, ensuring adequate allocation without project overloading.

### Step 4: Mitigate Conflicts of Interest & Establish Probity
Verify that nominated individuals do not perform dual, conflicting roles (e.g. client-side audit and delivery execution) and establish probity safeguards.

### Step 5: Formalize Nominations in Tender Return
Incorporate the nominated IM function sheet, organizational charts, and CVs into the formal tender return submission.

## Control Points & Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: Nominated lead has verified competence and at least 25-50% dedicated capacity allocated in writing.
- **Fail**: IM responsibilities assigned as an unresourced secondary duty to a full-time design manager.
