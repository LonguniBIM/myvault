# AI Fluency Framework

## Overview

The **4Ds Framework** is Anthropic's core methodology for developing AI fluency. It's taught in the "AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations" certification at Anthropic Academy.

## The 4Ds

1. **Delegation** - Knowing what to delegate to AI vs. what to handle yourself
2. **Description** - How to describe tasks clearly so AI understands intent
3. **Discernment** - Evaluating AI outputs critically; knowing when to trust or reject
4. **Diligence** - Verifying results, checking for hallucinations, validating accuracy
