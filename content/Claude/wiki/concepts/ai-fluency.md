# AI Fluency

## Overview

AI Fluency is the ability to work effectively with AI tools. It's not about knowing how to use a specific tool, but about developing judgment, critical thinking, and practical skills for AI-assisted work.

## Core Components

AI Fluency is built on the **4Ds Framework**:

1. **Delegation** - Knowing what to delegate to AI vs. what to handle yourself
2. **Description** - How to describe tasks clearly so AI understands intent
3. **Discernment** - Evaluating AI outputs critically; knowing when to trust or reject
4. **Diligence** - Verifying results, checking for hallucinations, validating accuracy

## Why It Matters

- **Market demand**: 78% of organizations used AI in 2024 (Stanford AI Index 2025)
- **Wage premium**: Workers with AI skills earn 56% wage premium (PwC 2025)
- **Career positioning**: AI fluency is increasingly expected in knowledge work

## Developing AI Fluency

1. **Learn the framework** - Understand the 4Ds
2. **Practice evaluation** - Develop taste training through repeated assessment
3. **Study examples** - Learn from high-quality AI interactions
4. **Build intuition** - Recognize patterns in AI behavior and output quality
5. **Stay current** - AI tools evolve; keep learning new capabilities
