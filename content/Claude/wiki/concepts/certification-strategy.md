# Certification Strategy

## Overview

Using AI tool certifications as credibility signals in the job market, particularly when <2% of the population has hands-on experience with advanced AI tools.

## Key Principles

1. **Signal value matters** - In a market where adoption is <2%, a certification proves you've actually used the tool
2. **Free credentials are stronger** - Paid certifications are often scams; official free ones from vendors carry more weight
3. **Visibility is required** - Completing a certification without adding it to LinkedIn wastes the signal value
4. **Wage premium is real** - Workers with AI skills earn 56% wage premium (PwC 2025), up from 25% in 2024
5. **Certification alone isn't sufficient** - But it's a necessary component of a credible AI skills profile

## Market Context (2024-2026)

- **78% of organizations** used AI in 2024 (Stanford AI Index 2025)
- **55%** used AI in 2023 → 23 percentage point jump in one year
- **56% wage premium** for workers with AI skills (PwC 2025)
- **25% wage premium** in 2024 → premium more than doubled in one year
- **<2% of people** have tried Claude Pro

## Implementation

**Where to add certifications:**
- LinkedIn profile → "Add section" → "Licenses & certifications"
- Format: Name, Issuing organization, Issue date

**Which certifications to pursue:**
- Only official Anthropic Academy certifications (anthropic.skilljar.com)
- Avoid paid "Claude Certification" courses (all scams)
