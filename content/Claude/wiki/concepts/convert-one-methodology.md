# Convert 1 (not train 10) methodology

The **"Convert 1 (not train 10)"** methodology is a strategic approach to AI adoption within teams. Instead of spending resources training 10 people on how to use AI generally, the focus is on **converting one specific business process** into a fully automated or assisted [[claude-skills|Claude Skill]].

## Core Principle

> "Don't train the person, digitize the process."

By creating a durable, high-quality Skill for a single repetitive task, the organization gains immediate, scalable efficiency that doesn't depend on the individual AI fluency of every team member.

## Role in Rollout

This concept is a cornerstone of the **[[5-day-playbook|5-Day Team Rollout Playbook]]** (typically scheduled for Thursday):
1. **Identify**: Find the most painful/repetitive process.
2. **Extract**: Use [[skill-creator]] to map the steps.
3. **Deploy**: Distribute the skill to the whole team.

## Benefits

- **Consistency**: Output quality remains high regardless of who triggers the skill.
- **Speed**: Onboarding new employees to a task becomes near-instant.
- **Scalability**: One "converted" process provides more value than 10 partially trained employees.

> Use [[makemyskill]] to refine the steps of the "one" converted process to ensure it matches the company's brand voice ([[design-md]]).
