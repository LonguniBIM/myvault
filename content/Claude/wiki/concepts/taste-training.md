# Taste Training

## Overview

Taste training is the process of developing judgment and discernment about AI output quality. It's about learning to recognize good vs. mediocre AI responses, knowing when to trust outputs, and understanding when to reject or refine them.

## Why It Matters

AI tools are only as good as your ability to evaluate their outputs. Without taste training, you can't distinguish between:
- Hallucinations vs. accurate information
- Generic vs. tailored responses
- Shallow vs. deep analysis
- Correct vs. plausible-sounding answers

## The 4Ds Framework

Taste training is built on the **4Ds** from [[AI Fluency Framework]]:

1. **Delegation** - Knowing what to delegate to AI vs. what to handle yourself
2. **Description** - How to describe tasks clearly so AI understands intent
3. **Discernment** - Evaluating AI outputs critically; knowing when to trust or reject
4. **Diligence** - Verifying results, checking for hallucinations, validating accuracy

## How to Develop Taste

1. **Study examples** - Read high-quality AI outputs and understand why they work
2. **Practice evaluation** - Regularly assess AI responses against your standards
3. **Learn patterns** - Recognize common failure modes and hallucination signatures
4. **Build intuition** - Over time, you develop gut feel for output quality
5. **Iterate** - Refine prompts based on feedback to improve outputs
