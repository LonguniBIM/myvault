# Anthropic Academy

## Overview

Anthropic Academy is Anthropic's official learning platform hosted at **anthropic.skilljar.com**. It provides free, official certifications for Claude users and AI practitioners.

## Key Facts

- **URL:** anthropic.skilljar.com
- **Cost:** Free (no Claude Pro subscription required)
- **Official status:** Only Anthropic-endorsed certifications; all others are scams
- **Audience:** Claude users, AI learners, professionals seeking AI fluency credentials

## Available Certifications

### 1. Claude 101
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Lessons:** 13 across 5 modules
- **Content:** Basics of Claude (Chat vs Cowork vs Code), Projects, Skills, connector setup
- **Connectors covered:** Gmail, Drive, Slack, and others
- **URL:** https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101

### 2. AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations
- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **Lessons:** 13
- **Content:** The 4Ds framework (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence), effective prompting, critical thinking, ethics
- **Key artifact:** AI Fluency Vocabulary Cheat Sheet (PDF, 1.43MB)
- **Author recommendation:** "If you only had time for 1 of the 3, take this one. It's the closest thing to taste training you'll find for free."
- **URL:** https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations

### 3. Introduction to Claude Cowork
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Content:** Cowork features, Projects, Plugins, Skills, scheduling, file handling, research at scale, permissions, model selection
- **Focus:** Practical Cowork usage
- **URL:** https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-claude-cowork
