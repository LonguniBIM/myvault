# Anthropic

## Overview

Anthropic is the AI research company that created [[Claude]]. Founded by former OpenAI researchers, Anthropic focuses on AI safety and building helpful, harmless, and honest AI systems.

## Products

- **[[Claude]]** - AI assistant (Chat, Cowork, Code)
- **[[Anthropic Academy]]** - Free certification platform (anthropic.skilljar.com)
- **Claude API** - Developer API for integrating Claude into applications

## Certifications

Anthropic offers three official free certifications through [[Anthropic Academy]]:
1. Claude 101 (1 hour)
2. AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (3 hours)
3. Introduction to Claude Cowork (2 hours)

**Important**: These are the only legitimate Claude certifications. All paid "Claude Certification" courses online are scams and not endorsed by Anthropic.
