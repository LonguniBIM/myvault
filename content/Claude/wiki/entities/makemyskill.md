# makemyskill.com (GPC)

**makemyskill.com** is a tool developed by **GPC** (Ruben Hassid's NY consulting firm) to streamline the creation of high-quality Claude Skills. It acts as a bridge between high-level business requirements and technical skill implementation.

## Functionality

- **Skill Drafting**: Provides a UI for inputting steps and triggers.
- **Optimization**: Applies Ruben Hassid's principles of "anti-AI writing" and "negative triggers" to skill design.
- **Community Bundles**: Allows users to share and download pre-built skill bundles (Plugins).

## Connection to Ruben Hassid

The tool implements the core methodologies taught in Ruben's [[claude-101-certification]] and [[ai-fluency-framework]].

> [!abstract] Business Value
> Enables companies to rapidly digitize their internal processes into [[claude-skills]], significantly reducing the training time for new hires (related to [[convert-one-methodology]]).
