# Get Claude Certified

## Overview

A complete workflow for acquiring all three official Anthropic Academy certifications in 6 hours and adding them to your LinkedIn profile for career signal value.

## Problem Solved

- **Scam avoidance**: Paid "Claude Certification" courses are scams; only Anthropic Academy certifications are legitimate
- **Career signaling**: In a market where <2% have tried Claude Pro, certifications prove hands-on experience
- **Wage premium**: Workers with AI skills earn 56% wage premium (PwC 2025)
- **Market demand**: 78% of organizations used AI in 2024 (Stanford AI Index 2025)

## Prerequisites

- Email address (no Claude Pro subscription required)
- 6 hours of time
- LinkedIn profile (for adding certifications)

## Time Estimate

**Total: 6 hours**
- Claude 101: 1 hour
- AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations: 3 hours
- Introduction to Claude Cowork: 2 hours

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Anthropic Academy

1. Go to **anthropic.skilljar.com**
2. Sign up with your email
3. No payment required (all certifications are free)

### Step 2: Take Claude 101 (1 hour)

1. Navigate to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101
2. Complete 13 lessons across 5 modules
3. **Content covered**:
   - What Claude is (Chat vs Cowork vs Code)
   - How Projects work
   - How Skills work
   - Connector setup (Gmail, Drive, Slack, etc.)
4. Complete the final assessment
5. Download your certificate

### Step 3: Take AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (3 hours)

1. Navigate to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations
2. Complete 13 lessons
3. **Content covered**:
   - The 4Ds framework: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence
   - Effective prompting techniques
   - Critical thinking around AI outputs
   - Ethics and responsible AI use
4. **Download the AI Fluency Vocabulary Cheat Sheet** (most valuable artifact)
5. Complete the final assessment
6. Download your certificate

**Note**: If you only have time for one certification, take this one. It's "the closest thing to taste training you'll find for free."

### Step 4: Take Introduction to Claude Cowork (2 hours)

1. Navigate to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-claude-cowork
2. Complete the course
3. **Content covered**:
   - What Cowork is and how to access it
   - Projects, Plugins, Skills
   - Scheduling tasks
   - File and document handling
   - Research at scale
   - Permissions
   - Model selection
4. Complete the final assessment
5. Download your certificate

### Step 5: Add Certifications to LinkedIn

For each of the three certifications:

1. Go to your LinkedIn profile
2. Click "Add section" → "Licenses & certifications"
3. Fill in the form using this exact format:

**For Claude 101:**
- **Name:** Claude 101 — Anthropic Academy
- **Issuing organization:** Anthropic
- **Issue date:** [today's date]
- **Credential ID:** [from your certificate]
- **Credential URL:** [from your certificate]

**For AI Fluency:**
- **Name:** AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations — Anthropic Academy
- **Issuing organization:** Anthropic
- **Issue date:** [today's date]
- **Credential ID:** [from your certificate]
- **Credential URL:** [from your certificate]

**For Claude Cowork:**
- **Name:** Introduction to Claude Cowork — Anthropic Academy
- **Issuing organization:** Anthropic
- **Issue date:** [today's date]
- **Credential ID:** [from your certificate]
- **Credential URL:** [from your certificate]

4. Click "Save" for each certification

## Expected Outcomes

- **3 official certifications** from Anthropic (the only legitimate Claude certifications)
- **LinkedIn profile enhancement** with credible AI skills signals
- **Career positioning** in the <2% who have hands-on Claude experience
- **Foundational knowledge** of Claude features, AI fluency, and Cowork workflows

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All 3 certifications completed and downloaded
- [ ] All 3 certifications added to LinkedIn profile
- [ ] AI Fluency Vocabulary Cheat Sheet downloaded and saved
- [ ] Profile shows "Licenses & certifications" section with Anthropic credentials

## Anti-Patterns

1. **Paying for certifications** - All legitimate Anthropic certifications are free; paid courses are scams
2. **Skipping AI Fluency** - It's the longest but most valuable; contains the best content
3. **Not adding to LinkedIn** - Completing without displaying wastes the signal value
4. **Expecting certification alone to get you hired** - It's a necessary signal, not sufficient alone
5. **Trusting non-Anthropic sources** - Only anthropic.skilljar.com certifications are endorsed

## Principles

1. **Authenticity over shortcuts** - Only Anthropic-issued certifications matter
2. **Free > Paid** - All legitimate certifications are free; paying is a red flag
3. **Signal value** - Certifications prove you're in the <2% with hands-on experience
4. **Taste training** - AI Fluency course develops judgment about AI quality
5. **Practical application** - Certifications should reflect actual tool usage
