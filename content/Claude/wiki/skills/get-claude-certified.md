# Get Claude Certified

## Why This Matters

In 2026, <2% of people have hands-on experience with Claude Pro. A certification proves you're in that elite group. Workers with AI skills earn a **56% wage premium** (PwC 2025), up from 25% in 2024. Adding official certifications to LinkedIn is a free, credible signal that you've actually used the tool.

**Important**: All paid "Claude Certification" courses online are scams. Only Anthropic Academy certifications are legitimate.

## The Skill

Acquire all three official Anthropic Academy certifications (6 hours total) and add them to your LinkedIn profile.

## Steps

### Phase 1: Access Anthropic Academy (5 minutes)

1. Go to **anthropic.skilljar.com**
2. Sign up with your email
3. No payment required

### Phase 2: Claude 101 (1 hour)

1. Go to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101
2. Complete 13 lessons (Chat vs Cowork vs Code, Projects, Skills, connectors)
3. Download your certificate

### Phase 3: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (3 hours)

1. Go to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations
2. Complete 13 lessons (4Ds framework, prompting, critical thinking, ethics)
3. **Download the AI Fluency Vocabulary Cheat Sheet** (most valuable artifact)
4. Download your certificate

**Pro tip**: If you only have time for one, take this one. It's the best.

### Phase 4: Introduction to Claude Cowork (2 hours)

1. Go to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-claude-cowork
2. Complete the course (Cowork features, Projects, Plugins, Skills, file handling)
3. Download your certificate

### Phase 5: Add to LinkedIn (15 minutes)

For each certification:

1. Go to your LinkedIn profile
2. Click "Add section" → "Licenses & certifications"
3. Fill in:
   - **Name:** [Certification name] — Anthropic Academy
   - **Issuing organization:** Anthropic
   - **Issue date:** [today]
   - **Credential ID/URL:** [from your certificate]
4. Save

**Repeat for all 3 certifications.**

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All 3 certifications completed
- [ ] All 3 added to LinkedIn
- [ ] AI Fluency Vocabulary Cheat Sheet saved for reference

## What You Get

- 3 official Anthropic certifications (the only legitimate ones)
- Credible LinkedIn profile enhancement
- Positioning in the <2% with hands-on Claude experience
- Foundational knowledge of Claude, AI fluency, and Cowork
- AI Fluency Vocabulary Cheat Sheet for ongoing reference

## Watch Out For

- **Paid courses** — All scams. Only anthropic.skilljar.com is legitimate.
- **Skipping AI Fluency** — It's the longest but most valuable.
- **Not adding to LinkedIn** — Completing without displaying wastes the signal.
- **Expecting it alone to get you hired** — It's necessary but not sufficient.
