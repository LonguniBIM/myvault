# Rollout Claude for Teams

## Why This Matters

Individual AI use is common, but team adoption is an "AI adoption failure." This 7-day playbook bypasses expensive consulting and IT tickets, focusing on converting an influential, overworked person first ("Sales Problem") rather than training the whole team at once.

## The Skill

Execute a 7-day rollout to move a team to a fully integrated Claude operation.

## Steps

### Day 1: Monday — Setup Projects

1. Identify 3-5 recurring deliverables (e.g., client memos, campaign briefs).
2. Create a shared **Claude Project** for each.
3. Upload "gold standard" examples and specific context (pricing, client rosters, brand guides) to each project.
4. Generate tailored instructions for each Project.

### Day 2: Tuesday — Prompt Templates

1. Create the shortest possible prompt template for each Project.
2. Structure: One sentence + one `[INPUT]` field.
3. Goal: Teammates should know exactly what to type without thinking.

### Day 3: Wednesday — The 'Wow' Moment

1. Perform a real, recurring task manually.
2. Perform the same task via the Project.
3. Document before/after results (time saved, quality diff) and take screenshots as proof of value.

### Day 4: Thursday — Convert the Champion

1. Onboard one colleague who is "drowning" in work and influential.
2. Run a 15-minute session using their *actual* current work.
3. Demonstrate the "wow" effect live.
4. Make them a co-owner of the Project.

### Day 5: Friday — Full Rollout

1. Send a casual, result-led message to the team channel (Slack/Teams) showing the "Wow" moment.
2. Separately DM 2-3 specific people and ask them to try a specific template.
3. Collect feedback at the end of the day.

### Weekend/Next Week: Feedback Loop

1. Use team feedback to adjust instructions.
2. Add new templates based on requested use cases.
3. Address misconceptions (e.g., security, hallucination).

## Watch Out For

- **Training everyone at once**: Results in low conversion. Start with 1.
- **Training the skeptic**: Wrong leverage. Convert the champion instead.
- **Context Bloat**: Sales proposals don't need brand guides. Give Projects *only* what they need.
- **Corporate Language**: Kills engagement. Keep it casual and results-led.
