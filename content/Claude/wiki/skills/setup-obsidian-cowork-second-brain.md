# Setup Obsidian Cowork Second Brain

## Why This Matters

Managing raw `.md` files in basic text editors is frustrating and geeky. Obsidian makes your AI's context files look like a knowledge base, making it easy to keep your "brain" up to date.

## The Skill

Link Obsidian to your Cowork folder for live-syncing context management.

## Steps

### 1. Install Obsidian

1. Download **Obsidian** from obsidian.md.
2. Install and launch the app.

### 2. Connect to Cowork

1. In Obsidian, click **"Open folder as a vault."**
2. Select your root `Claude Cowork` folder.
3. You now see your `about-me`, `claude-output`, and `templates` folders in a clean sidebar.

### 3. Management Workflow

- **Search**: Use `Ctrl+F` in Obsidian to search through everything you or Claude have ever written.
- **Edit**: Edit your `about-me.md` or `my-company.md` like a Google Doc. Changes sync live to your computer folder.
- **Refresh**: Start a new Cowork session, point to the folder, and Claude instantly reads your latest updates.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Obsidian sidebar reflects your Cowork folder structure.
- [ ] Successfully edited a context file in Obsidian and verified the update in a Claude session.
- [ ] Found a past deliverable using Obsidian search.

## Watch Out For

- **File Formats**: Ensure files are saved as `.md` or `.txt`.
- **Cloud Sync**: Obsidian is local. If you want it on other devices, use Obsidian Sync or a cloud-synced folder (but ensure Cowork can still see the local path).
