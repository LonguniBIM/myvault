## Six-Question Analysis

### Q1 — What problem does this solve?

**Problem:** People are being scammed by fake "Claude Certification" courses sold online, while legitimate free certifications from Anthropic exist but are unknown to most users.

**Secondary problems:**
- Job seekers need credible AI skills signals on their profiles
- Workers need to demonstrate AI fluency to capture wage premiums (56% according to PwC)
- Organizations need employees with verified AI tool competency (78% of orgs used AI in 2024)

### Q2 — What numbered steps does the author prescribe?

**Primary Method: Getting Claude Certified (3 certifications in 6 hours)**

**Step 1: Access the right website**
1. Go to anthropic.skilljar.com
2. Sign up with your email
3. No Claude Pro subscription required

**Step 2: Take Claude 101 first (1 hour)**
1. Go to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101
2. Complete 13 lessons across 5 modules
3. Covers: Chat vs Cowork vs Code, Projects, Skills, connector setup (Gmail, Drive, Slack)

**Step 3: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (3 hours)**
1. Go to https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations
2. Complete 13 lessons on AI engagement
3. Learn the 4Ds: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence
4. Download the vocabulary cheat sheet (most valuable artifact)
5. **Author's recommendation:** If you only have time for one, take this one

**Step 4: Introduction to Claude Cowork (2 hours)**
1. Complete the Cowork-specific certification
2. Covers: Projects, Plugins, Skills, scheduling, file handling, research at scale, permissions, model selection

**Adding to LinkedIn:**
1. Go to your profile
2. Click "Add section" → "Licenses & certifications"
3. Use exact format:
   - **Name:** Claude 101 — Anthropic Academy
   - **Issuing organization:** Anthropic
   - **Issue date:** today
4. Repeat for AI Fluency and Introduction to Claude Cowork

### Q3 — What principles recur?

1. **Authenticity over shortcuts** - Only Anthropic-issued certifications matter; paid courses are scams
2. **Free > Paid** - All legitimate certifications are free; paying is a red flag
3. **Signal value** - Certifications serve as credibility signals in a market where <2% have tried Claude Pro
4. **Taste training** - AI Fluency course is "the closest thing to taste training you'll find for free"
5. **Practical application** - Certifications should reflect actual tool usage, not theoretical knowledge
6. **Career pragmatism** - $0 cost + few hours = clean profile line showing you're learning current tools

### Q4 — What mistakes does the author warn against?

1. **Paying for fake certifications** - "People are selling viral 'Claude Certifications' online right now. They are all scams."
2. **Trusting non-Anthropic sources** - "Anthropic does not endorse a single one of them"
3. **Skipping the AI Fluency course** - It's the longest but also the best; contains the most valuable content
4. **Not adding to LinkedIn** - Completing the cert without displaying it wastes the signal value
5. **Assuming certification alone gets you hired** - "Will it get you hired alone? Probably not." But it's a necessary signal.

### Q5 — What diagnostic questions does the author pose?

1. "Why should you even care?" → Because 78% of organizations used AI in 2024 (up from 55% in 2023)
2. "Will it get you hired alone?" → No, but it provides a clean credibility signal
3. Implicit: "Are you in the <2% who have actually tried Claude Pro?" → The certification proves you are
