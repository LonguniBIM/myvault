## Six-Question Analysis

### Q1 — What problem does this solve?

It solves the "AI adoption gap" for the 98.75% of the population who have never tried Claude. It explains AI concepts in plain English, reduces beginner frustration, and provides a clear onboarding path for those moving from ChatGPT or starting from zero.

### Q2 — What numbered steps does the author prescribe?

**Onboarding Workflow:**
1. Sign up at **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai/)** (start with the Free plan to test, move to Pro for daily use).
2. Install the **desktop app** (Mac/Windows) to unlock file access and Cowork.
3. Start with **Chat** mode for drafts and thinking out loud.
4. Graduate to **Cowork** (Pro plan) for multi-step jobs (e.g., cleaning 40 invoices at once).
5. Book 20 minutes this week to try 3 of the "10 things to try" (e.g., meeting notes decisions log, Downloads folder cleanup, competitor pricing analysis).

**Prompting Rules:**
1. **Be specific**: Vague prompts = vague output.
2. **Give examples**: Paste your own writing; Claude learns faster from examples than instructions.
3. **Say what you want**: Positive instructions are more effective than negative ones.
4. **Start short, add detail**: Treat it as a conversation (chat) rather than a one-shot command.
5. **Fresh start**: If Claude gets confused or the context window fills up, start a new chat.

### Q3 — What principles recur?

- **AI as an Employee**: Claude is a "very good employee" that needs the direction of a boss (you).
- **Files over Prompts**: Context (information before a task) is best provided via local folders and files.
- **Discernment/Taste**: Claude is average by default; the user's "taste" and context are what make the output superior.

### Q4 — What mistakes does the author warn against?

- **Trusting agreement**: Claude is trained to be helpful (sycophancy) and might nod along even if you are wrong.
- **Using Claude for precise math**: It's an auto-complete machine, not a calculator (unless it's running code).
- **Vague requests**: Assuming Claude is an "otherworld god" that knows what you want without detail.
- **Relying on Claude for real-time info**: Without search active, it will hallucinate current events confidently.

### Q5 — What diagnostic questions does the author pose?

- Are you in the 1.25% who have actually tried Claude?
- Did you start a fresh chat after it got confused?
- Are you writing a 500-word prompt on your first try (don't) or starting short?

### Q6 — Can the method be expressed as numbered steps?
