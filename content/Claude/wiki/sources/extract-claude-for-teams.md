## Six-Question Analysis

### Q1 — What problem does this solve?

It solves the "AI adoption failure" problem in organizations. While individuals may use AI, teams often struggle to integrate it into recurring workflows. This guide provides a 7-day playbook to move a team from "pretending to use AI" to a fully integrated Claude-powered operation without expensive consulting or IT tickets.

### Q2 — What numbered steps does the author prescribe?

**The 7-Day Rollout Playbook:**
1. **Monday (Setup Projects)**: Use Claude to identify 3-5 recurring deliverables (e.g., client memos, campaign briefs). Create a shared Claude Project for each, uploading "gold standard" examples and specific context (pricing, client rosters). Generate tailored instructions for each.
2. **Tuesday (Prompt Templates)**: Create the shortest possible prompt template (one sentence, one `[INPUT]` field) for each Project so teammates know exactly what to type.
3. **Wednesday (The 'Wow' Moment)**: Perform a real task manually vs. via the Project. Document the before/after results and screenshots as proof of value.
4. **Thursday (Convert the Champion)**: Onboard one colleague who is "drowning" in work. Run a 15-minute session using their actual work to demonstrate the "wow" effect. Make them a co-owner.
5. **Friday (Full Rollout)**: Send a casual, result-led Slack message to the team channel. Separately DM 2-3 people to try a specific template. Collect feedback at the end of the day.
6. **Weekend/Next Week (Feedback Loop)**: Use team feedback to adjust instructions, add new templates, and address misconceptions.

### Q3 — What principles recur?

- **Adoption is a Sales Problem**: Don't train 10 people; convert 1 influential, overworked person first.
- **Projects over Cowork (for teams)**: Use Claude Cowork for personal tasks, but use **Claude Projects** for teams to provide a "source of truth" and shared context.
- **Context Isolation**: Sales proposals don't need brand guides; meeting recaps don't need pricing. Each Project gets *only* the context it needs.
- **intelligence Compounds**: Humans evolve by being smart together. Team AI adoption is the modern version of "hunting together".

### Q4 — What mistakes does the author warn against?

- Trying to train the whole team at once (low conversion).
- Training the biggest skeptic or the tech enthusiast first (wrong leverage).
- Giving too much context (bloats the Project and confuses the AI).
- Using formal, corporate language in the rollout (kills engagement).

### Q5 — What diagnostic questions does the author pose?

- What tasks feel repetitive every week or month?
- What work always ends up being redone because the first version wasn't right?
- Who on the team is visibly drowning or behind on deliverables?
