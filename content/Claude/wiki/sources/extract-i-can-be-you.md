## Six-Question Analysis

### Q1 — What problem does this solve?

It solves the "generic AI output" and "re-explanation fatigue" problem. Most people struggle because Claude doesn't know their "voice" or "taste" by default. This guide provides a 2-hour workflow to extract your unique DNA (beliefs, mechanics, cringes) into a portable text file that turns any AI into a version of you.

### Q2 — What numbered steps does the author prescribe?

**The "Extract Yourself" Workflow**:
1. **The 100-Question Interview**:
   - Use Claude (Opus 4.7 + Extended Thinking) + Wispr Flow (for voice speed).
   - Prompt Claude as a "Taste Interviewer" to ask 100 deep questions across categories: Beliefs, Mechanics, Aesthetic Crimes, Voice, Structure, Hard NOs, and Red Flags.
   - Answer verbatim (takes ~90-120 minutes).
2. **The Compression Step**:
   - Use a "Voice Compiler" prompt to turn the 20,000-word transcript into a compact, high-signal `<about_me>` XML/Markdown file (2,000–4,000 tokens).
   - This file prioritizes "behavioral fidelity" over biographical summary.
3. **The Deployment**:
   - Save the `.md` file in a local folder.
   - Point Claude Cowork to this folder or paste the content as "standing context" for every new session.
4. **The Maintenance Loop**:
   - Use **Obsidian** (free) to edit your `.md` profile like a Google Doc.
   - Update it often as your taste evolves or as you spot new patterns you want to capture.

### Q3 — What principles recur?

- **Patterns are DNA**: Your identity is just a collection of patterns (words you delete, analogies you repeat, cringes you have).
- **Portable Identity**: Once you are a text file, you are "compatible" with any AI, team member, or ghostwriter.
- **Voice > Bio**: The AI doesn't need your life story; it needs your "writing laws" and "aesthetic crimes" to emulate your judgment.
- **Vagueness is the Enemy**: If a line doesn't change how the AI decides or writes, cut it.

### Q4 — What mistakes does the author warn against?

- **Stopping at the Transcript**: Uploading a 20,000-word interview is too token-heavy and expensive. Compression is mandatory.
- **Generic Values**: Using words like "simple" or "clear" without defining what they look like in practice.
- **Bio-padding**: Including biographical details that don't actually affect the output (e.g., where you went to school, unless it shaped your vocabulary).
- **Static Profiles**: Failing to edit the file as your taste changes (leading to "predictable" or "stale" AI versions of yourself).

### Q5 — What diagnostic questions does the author pose?

- If this line disappeared, would the AI write, edit, judge, or decide differently? (The compression test).
- Am I chasing validation or truth? (The discernment test).
- Does this sound like the "cringe" version of me or the "real" version?
