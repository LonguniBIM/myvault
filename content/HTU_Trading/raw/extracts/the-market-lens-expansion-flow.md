## Visual Description

Infographic titled **"themarketlens.com — expansion model"** — a decision-flow chart mapping the 4-layer filter of the Expansion Model from Caution → Structure → Invalidation → Execution → Into Objectives.

## Caution Protocol Layer

| Node | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Caution Protocol** | Understand the specific rules to avoid losing trades in the market |
| **Avoid Monday** | No opening trades on Monday within any given week |
| **High Impact News** | Consumer Price Index, Non-Farm Payroll, FOMC Press Conference |
| **If This, Then That** | Entries can be opened on Free Monday: pointed back with black line following the high-impact news day; P5 |
| **News Protocol** | Entries can be opened any time after the high-impact news has entered the market |

## Structure Layer

| Node | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Relevant Swings** | Look for failure swings as areas in price using the daily and 30 daily candle lookback |
| **Protected Swing** | Bullish: reverse leg from a swing low, high or low. Failure swing to the high or low |
| **Close Through** | Look to either the daily high or the daily low; both are extremes |
| **Neutral Range** | Failure swings to both sides of the market meaning price remains at equilibrium |
| **Large Wick** | Reversal day can be created by a prior day with failure swing as the relevant level; adding conviction to the current trading day high or low |
| **Swing Breakout** | Expansion can be signaled from breaking out of current day range to the high or low |
| **Internal Range** | Price swings to either the external or Internal range means price is within the external range to be engaged |
| **External Range** | Sales within a protected swing to either the external or internal range means price is at the range to be engaged |

## Invalidation Layer

| Node | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Invalidation Point** | Determine invalidation point on the reversal day or continuation day |
| **Reversal Day** | Invalidation dependent on how price traded at or near the reversal day high or low |
| **Continuation Day** | Invalidation point set from previous reversal high or low or beyond trading from one candle |
| **Invalidated Day** | Price trades through at any point on the day at or later than the 01:00 reversal |
| **Relevant Level** | Annotate the relevant daily high or low or the correct timing bar |
| **Large Candle Wick** | Equilibrium of previous day large wick becomes the invalidation |
| **Respecting** | How price has a low price momentum level at time of engaging the relevant swing |
| **Large Candle Body** | Equilibrium of previous day closes away from becomes the invalidation |
| **Remaining Valid** | Price remains above price level when bearish or below price level when bearish (sic) |

## Execution Layer

| Node | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Profile Confirmation** | Track development of the three daily profile ratios in relation to the frameworks |
| **01:00 Reversal** | If the 18:00 profile forms the high or low of day, then wait till 01:00 candle to confirm |
| **08:00 Reversal** | If no reversal forms during 01:00 candle, wait for reversal in 08:00 candle |
| **01:00 Confirmation** | 5:00 candle closing confirmation on 30m or 15m for initial alignment to the 01:00 candle open |
| **01:00 Entry** | First valid continuation entry signature on 30m or 15m following the 01:00 candle open |
| **08:00 Confirmation** | 08:00 candle closing confirmation on 15m or 5m to start the 08:00 confirmation |
| **08:00 Entry** | First valid confirmation entry signature on 15m or 5m following the 08:00 candle open |
| **Overnight Expansion** | When after 18:00 and 01:00 candles have traded, confirmation expands significantly towards the desired direction |
| **Large Wick** | After a large opposing run has formed, look for the large wick following the close through criteria for trades that become unfavourable |

## Into Objectives

Target framework based on previous highs/lows and equilibrium of large ranges on D/4H/1H timeframes.
