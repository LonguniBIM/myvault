## A. Expansion Model (AM / The Market Lens)

### Section 1: Economic Calendar

**Calendar Settings**: Remove orange/yellow folder events. Only red folder (high impact) + gray folder (market closure reminders). Filter currencies relevant to market traded (USD for indices/gold/oil; pair currencies for forex).

**News Protocol**:
- Never open trades any session PRIOR to a high impact news event
- Never hold an open position through a high impact news release
- Be aware of all red folder events on trading days (can be used for confluence)
- High Impact Events: Consumer Price Index M/M, Non-Farm Payroll, FOMC Press Conference
- Data: Day prior to high impact has only 7% below-average range — no need to avoid

### Section 2: Price Signatures

**Failure Swings**: Areas where market failed to properly reverse from.
- Failure swing highs: initial high → price moves deep back up but fails to exceed → lower highs build
- Failure swing lows: initial low → price moves deep back down but fails to breach → higher lows build
- Relevant swing = highest high (failure high) or lowest low (failure low)
- False failure swings: If correlated pair shows manipulation at same point → divergence, not neutral

**Protected Swings**: Points tradeable in opposing direction after manipulation or divergence.

Manipulation:
- Bullish: trades below relevant low → quickly returns above → confirmed by close above open of highest downclose candle that ran the low
- Bearish: trades above relevant high → quickly returns below → confirmed by close below open of lowest upclose candle that ran the high
- HTF invalidation: close through 50% level of highest body to lowest body in series
- Entry invalidation: trade through the protected swing point itself

Divergence (when manipulation absent):
- Correlated pair shows failure swing where primary pair shows manipulation
- Divergence low confirmation: close above open of highest downclose candle forming the higher low
- Divergence high confirmation: close below open of lowest upclose candle forming the lower high
- Protected swing = lower low (divergence low) or higher high (divergence high) on pair with failure swing

Opposing Candles (in continuation):
- Downclose candles support price when bullish; upclose candles resist when bearish
- Select: single or series with bulky body + wide range
- Ignore: small range candle body/wick series
- Invalidation same as manipulation rules above

### Section 3: Caution Protocol

**Monday Rule**: Never trade Monday.
- Smallest average daily range of the week
- No high impact news events on Monday
- No weekly context yet — speculation highest

**Expansion Rule**: 3 consecutive expansion daily candles in same direction → phase complete.
- Stop trading continuations in previous direction
- Reestablish bias; anticipate new phase of price delivery
- Valid expansion candle: larger-than-average range + bulky candle body

**Neutral Range**: Failure swings to both sides + price at equilibrium of the two failure swings.
- Mark highest high and lowest low from the failure swings
- Protocol: wait for price to engage one of the two extremes
  - Manipulation at extreme: shallow run → intraday trade; extended run → next-day continuation
  - Continuation through extreme: daily candle close through level + hourly opposing close candles confirm

### Section 4: Actionable Market

**Lookback Period**: 30 daily candles only. Only objectives can be outside this window.

**Valid Framework**:
- Daily → Hourly timeframe alignment
- Three scenarios: Close Proximity Swing, Confirmed Protected Swing, Opposing Draw
- Opposing draw = failure swings on other side of market as objective (ideal; less resistance)

**Weekly Range**: New week = reset bias. Look for initial confirmed protected swing within week and trade opposing direction until invalidated, objective reached, or expansion rule triggered.

### Section 5: Intraday Confirmation (Daily Profiles)

**Previous Day Framework**:
- Large Wick Day: mark equilibrium of wick (open to low/high) → this is invalidation line
- Expansion Day: mark equilibrium of full range (low to high or high to low) → this is invalidation line
- Hourly refinement: find opposing candles on H1 near invalidation point → refine to midpoint of those candles

**7-Hour Chart**: Three 7H candles per day:
| Candle | Time | Session | Reversal % | Range % | Same-dir close % |
|--------|------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| 18:00 | 18:00–01:00 | Asia | 40% | 17% | 58% |
| 01:00 | 01:00–08:00 | London | 21% | 24% | 62% |
| 08:00 | 08:00–15:00 | New York | 39% | 59% | 83% |

**Daily Profiles**:
1. **18:00 Reversal**: 18:00 forms opposing swing; no trading within 01:00 candle if swing intact; confirm 08:00 is too far to return → first continuation entry after 08:00 open
2. **01:00 Reversal**: 18:00 ranges/makes opposing run; 01:00 runs out 18:00 opposing swing; reversal confirmation → continuation entries in 01:00 or 08:00
3. **08:00 Reversal**: Both 18:00 and 01:00 range/opposing run; 08:00 runs out both; reversal + continuation within 08:00

Full Invalidation: If 18:00 or 01:00 expands significantly (all objectives taken out or expansion through invalidation point).

**Entry Logic**:
- 01:00 candle: 30m and 15m timeframes
- 08:00 candle: 15m and 5m timeframes
- Initial Reversal: Change in state of delivery (close above lowest downclose candles or below highest upclose candles)
- Continuation Entry: Close through opposing candles with bulky body + clear close
- Confluences: Run on short-term swing, divergence at opposing swing, opposing candle at volatility driver, close through at volatility driver
- Entry: Market order on close through (not limit order)
- Stop loss: on swing point of other side of opposing candles (protected swing)

### Section 6: Trade Management

**Position Sizing**: Fixed dollar risk (not fixed point). Adjust contracts to fixed dollar amount.

**Objectives and Scaling**:
- Minimum 2R before taking profit
- Initial objective = absolute minimum expectation; if not achieved, idea is wrong
- Target types: Previous highs/lows + equilibrium of large ranges on D/4H/1H
- Scale off half+ at first objective beyond 2R
- Rule: Never let 2R+ profit turn to breakeven or loss

**Trailing Stop Loss**:
- No early breakeven moves; wait for new invalidation point
- Trail using opposing close candles (bulky, confirmed) on 15m/5m
- If aggressive expansion (no opposing candles): favor scaling over trailing; or trail by RR
- Begin active management only after 1.5R

**Risk Management**:
- Determine max consecutive losses from personal data
- Risk per trade: ensure worst-case streak still leaves buffer
- Intraday Rule: Maximum 2 losses per day (indisputable)

## B. Fractal Model (TTrades)

Core concepts: Opposing candles, Fair Value Gaps, Equilibrium, CISD (Change in State of Delivery), Projections.

Swing formation: Candle 1 (before H/L), Candle 2 (forms H/L), Candle 3 (follows), Candle 4 (continuation).
- Candle 2 closure: closes inside C1 range = reversal → C3 expansion anticipated
- Candle 3 closure: closes through open of C2 = validates swing → C4 continuation
- CISD required: Lower timeframe confirmation (weekly→4H, daily→1H, 4H→15m, 1H→5m, 30m→3m, 15m→1m)
- T-Spot: 0.5 of CISD + 0.5 of EQ = where higher timeframe wick forms
- IC-CISD: Intra-candle CISD confirms wick has formed → entry signal

Timeframes: Daily/4H for bias; 1H-M5 for fractal entries; M30/M3 for smaller fractal.

Entry types: Candle 2 closure entry (higher RR, less confirmed), Candle 3 closure entry (confirmed), CISD/Opposing candle entry.

## C. GxT Model (Garrett)

Based on Fractal Model (prerequisite). Focus: 4-hour profiling + OHLC/OLHC candle logic.

OLHC (bullish): Open → Low (wick) → High → Close. Small wick = expansion supported.
OHLC (bearish): Open → High (wick) → Low → Close.

Sessions:
- London Reversal: 2am–5am low formation at relevant level
- NY Continuation: 8am–10am or 10am–12pm continuation away from LOD
- NY Reversal: 6am or 10am reversal forming LOD/HOD

4H Profile: 2am Reversal → 6am Continuation; 6am Reversal → 10am Continuation; 10am Reversal.

Targets: IRL→ERL, ERL→IRL, HRLR→LRLR order pairing ranges.
